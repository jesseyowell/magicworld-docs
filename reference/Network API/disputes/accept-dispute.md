title: Accept dispute
excerpt: >-
  Resolves a dispute by having the merchant accept responsibility for it. This
  leads to a "loss" of the dispute from the merchant's perspective, and a "win"
  of the dispute from the customer's perspective.


  > **Note:** Once a dispute is accepted, the decision is final; it can never be
  changed.


  **This endpoint is not rate limited.**


  Scopes: `DISPUTES_WRITE`
